Key Insights
Revenue Mix
Premium projects (€8k) + Standard projects (€7k) = €15k in project revenue. October is the peak revenue month at €7,500.
Cost Structure
Event costs (kick-off, networking, gala) represent the largest single cost block. Operations are lean and spread evenly at €245/month.
Legal Position
6 paid contracts to review per semester. Insurance secured in September. Compliance filing due in December.
Cash Flow
Positive net balance expected in Sep, Oct and Nov. Largest inflow in October 1 premium + 2 standard projects closing simultaneously.
Risk Flags
Sponsorship base is small (3 sponsors). Revenue concentrated in Oct–Nov. Pro-bono projects offer no direct financial return.
Opportunities
Closing the €5k premium deal would lift revenue by €1k (+5.2%). Growing the sponsor base from 3 to 5 is the key commercial lever.
